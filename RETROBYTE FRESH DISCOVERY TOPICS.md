# RetroByte fresh discovery queue

These subjects are new to the published RetroByte catalog. Once a title is
generated or posted, it is retired permanently; add a genuinely new subject
here when this queue runs out.

## 01 — The PalmPilot
Angle: A pocket organizer that let people tap appointments and notes with a tiny stylus.

## 02 — The MiniDisc
Angle: A small cartridge protected a rewritable digital music disc you could record again and again.

## 03 — The LaserDisc
Angle: A shiny video disc the size of a record spun inside a player to show a movie.

## 04 — The CD-ROM Encyclopedia
Angle: One disc could hold an illustrated reference library that used to fill whole shelves.
